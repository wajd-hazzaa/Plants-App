<h3 align="center">A Little Reminder to Keep Your Plants Happy 🌿</h3>
<p align="center">
  Planto is a plant care reminder app that helps users organize their plants, manage watering schedules, and track their daily plant care progress.
</p>

⸻

✨ Features

* 🌱 Add Plants: Add plants by entering their name and selecting their room, light requirements, watering frequency, and water amount.
* ✏️ Edit Plants: Update plant information whenever needed.
* 🗑️ Delete Plants: Remove plants from your collection.
* 📊 Daily Progress: Track daily plant care activities through a progress bar.
* 💧 Mark as Completed: Mark a plant as watered once its care task is completed.
* 🔔 Local Notifications: Receive watering reminders to maintain a consistent plant care routine.
* 🪴 Plant Organization: View and manage all your plants and their care details in one place.

⸻

🛠️ Technologies Used

* Swift – Programming language
* SwiftUI – User interface development
* UserDefaults – Local data storage
* UserNotifications – Local notifications
* MVVM – Application architecture

⸻

🎨 Design

Planto features a clean and modern dark interface with green accents. The design focuses on simplicity and usability, providing a calm and intuitive experience that makes everyday plant care easier.
